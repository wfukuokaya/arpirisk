# arpirisk: ARPI Treatment Effects by Baseline Risk in Metastatic Prostate Cancer

Predicts the 4-year risk of all-cause death with androgen deprivation
therapy alone in patients with metastatic hormone-sensitive prostate
cancer, and estimates the treatment effects on overall survival of
adding an androgen receptor pathway inhibitor at that risk: the
difference in 4-year survival probability, the restricted mean survival
time difference through 4 years, and the hazard ratio. The model was
developed from individual participant data of 4 randomized clinical
trials.
