# mhspcarpi: ARPI Treatment Effects by Baseline Risk in Metastatic Prostate Cancer

Implements a risk model, developed from individual participant data of 4
placebo-controlled randomized clinical trials, that predicts the 4-year
risk of all-cause death with placebo plus androgen deprivation therapy
in patients with metastatic hormone-sensitive prostate cancer. The
package uses this baseline risk to report pooled estimates of the
treatment effects of androgen receptor pathway inhibitors on overall
survival: the difference in 4-year survival probability, the restricted
mean survival time difference through 4 years, and the hazard ratio.
